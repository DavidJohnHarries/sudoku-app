tip Start with the green loop. A 5 can never sit on a German whisper, so the 16 cells alternate low (1-4) and high (6-9). The loop has even length, and the four corners are an even number of steps apart, so they all have the same polarity — all low, or all high.
tip Each pink line is a 5-cell renban that sits entirely in one box and includes one corner of the green loop. A 5-cell renban always contains a 5, and that 5 cannot be the corner on the whisper, so it has to be one of the other four cells. Use that, and the dots in those two boxes, to decide the polarity of the corners.
tip Once you know the polarity, look at the white dots on the sides of the green rectangle, especially in row 4. Each one joins a cell just outside the loop to a cell on it, and that tightens both the loop and the two renbans.
