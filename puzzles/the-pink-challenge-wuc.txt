tip Start with the two long 6-cell renbans. A 6-cell line has only four possible sets: 1-6, 2-7, 3-8 or 4-9. The 21-cage sits on the lower line, so that one cannot be 1-6: its two cage cells would be too small to make 21. Also notice that r3c7 sees every cell of the upper line, and r7c3 sees every cell of the lower line, so neither cell can take a digit that appears on its line.
tip Next look at the 4-cell hooks in box 3 and box 7. Each hook lies entirely inside its box and shares a cell with a cage (the 5-cage up top, the 15-cage down below). The other cell of that cage still has to fit in the same box, beside the whole hook set, and that rules out most of the remaining 6-cell options.
tip After the two long lines are pinned down, the 1 on the upper line has to sit in row 3, because column 7 already has a 1. Use that in the 13-cage, and on the lower line look at which high pairs can still make the 21-cage.
