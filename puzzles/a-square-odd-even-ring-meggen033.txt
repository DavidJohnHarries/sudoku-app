tip The grey circles (odd) and squares (even) sit exactly on the 16-cell ring around the centre: six odd cells and ten even cells. By Phistomefel, the four 2×2 corners of the grid contain the same 16 digits, so they also have six odd and ten even.
tip The top-right 2×2 (r1c8–r2c9) has an X on each row, so each of those pairs sums to 10 and the four cells sum to 20. The bottom-left 2×2 (r8c1–r9c2) is the same. The top-left 2×2 has a 12-cage on r1c2–r2c2 and a white (consecutive) dot on r1c1–r2c1; the bottom-right 2×2 is the mirror of that (12-cage r8c8–r9c8, white dot r8c9–r9c9).
tip The two black dots on the ring join even cells: r3c6–r3c7 and r7c3–r7c4, so those pairs are 2–4 or 4–8. The 13-cages r4c8–r5c8 and r5c2–r6c2 are 4–9, 5–8 or 6–7. Two cages have no total — they only say the digits don't repeat: r3c4/r4c3/r4c4/r5c4, and r6c6/r6c7/r7c6 (the last two of those are odd cells on the ring).
