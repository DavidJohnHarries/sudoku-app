Tap (or drag from) the circle, then the shaft cells in order to the arrowhead. Tap the last cell again to remove it. “Circle” sets a 1-, 2- or 3-cell circle (2 cells = a 2-digit number). With nothing selected, tap an arrow to edit or delete it.

Tap near the line between two cells to cycle: no mark → white dot (consecutive) → black dot (one is double the other) → X (sum 10) → V (sum 5) → none. “All dots given” and “All X/V given” are negative constraints: only turn them on if the rules say every such clue is shown.

Tap a cell to cycle: no mark → grey circle (odd: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9) → grey square (even: 2, 4, 6, 8) → none.
